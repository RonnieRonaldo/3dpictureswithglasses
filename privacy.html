Privacy & analytics

You can enjoy the 3D art gallery and follow the book links whether you accept or reject analytics.

Optional Google Analytics

If you select “Accept analytics”, we load Google Analytics 4 to understand visits and use of the website. Google may process cookie identifiers, pages visited, browser and device information, approximate location and interactions. Analytics cookies can recognise a browser across visits.

Our Google Analytics script does not load before you accept. Advertising storage, advertising user data and advertising personalisation consent remain off. You can reject analytics or change your choice using “Analytics settings” below.

Withdrawing consent disables further collection through our Google Analytics tag and removes accessible first-party Google Analytics cookies for this site. It does not erase information already collected by Google. Read how Google uses information from sites using its services.

Remembering your choice

We store your analytics choice in your browser’s local storage for up to 180 days. This preference is separate from analytics cookies. If storage is blocked, your choice may not persist between pages. Clearing this site’s browser data removes the saved choice. Closing the banner does not grant consent or change an existing choice.

The 3D art gallery

The gallery and fullscreen image viewer works without optional analytics. It does not require an account or payment. This website does not ask you to submit personal information to use the preview.

Bookshops and other websites

Links to Apple Books, Google Play Books and Dino 3D Adventure take you to separate websites with their own privacy settings. Purchases are handled by the bookshop you choose. This gallery website does not collect your payment card details.

Website delivery

Cloudflare hosts and delivers this website. Technical request data, including IP addresses, is processed to deliver and protect the site. This is separate from optional Google Analytics. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy.
